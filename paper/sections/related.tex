\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Correction-based safeguards.} Compression
Safeguards~\cite{tyree2026safeguards} wrap any compressor with declarative
requirements (pointwise bounds, sign preservation relative to an offset,
bounds on quantities of interest over stencils) and enforce them by
computing corrections after compression. A window-average threshold test on
a regular grid can be expressed as a stencil QoI with the comparison itself
as the quantity and bound zero, which is how we run it as a baseline
(\cref{sec:eval-sg}). The differences are in what must be translated and
how it is enforced. Hold durations need no translation for our criterion
(\cref{lem:automaton}), but phase robustness does: a stencil fixes the
window length in samples, so missed scrapes and unknown evaluation phases
require one stencil per length seen and truncated windows at the edges.
Prometheus' \code{rate} extrapolation and counter resets
(\cref{prop:rate}) have no stencil form; our translation (reset-corrected
increase over the sampled interval) changes decisions on gappy counters.
Safeguards correct every violating point; our analytic budget is a sound
start that needs no correction, and the optimistic variant corrects only
flipped supports with the analytic budget as the floor.

\paragraph{Quantity-of-interest preservation.} Jiao et
al.~\cite{jiao2022qoi} derive pointwise error bounds from bounds on derived
quantities by error propagation; our Lipschitz step is an instance. We bound
\emph{decisions} rather than the error of a quantity, which makes the
budget depend on the margin to a threshold, and we add the temporal
structure (overlapping windows, phase, the alert automaton).

\paragraph{Feature- and topology-preserving compression.} TopoSZ and its
successors~\cite{yan2024toposz} preserve critical points and contour trees
of scientific fields; adaptive schemes tighten bounds near detected
features~\cite{adaptivefeature2024}. Level-set preservation at a level
$\theta$ is sign preservation of $x - \theta$, the \code{last} special case
of our setting; window aggregates and alert state have no counterpart there.

\paragraph{Time-series compression.} Gorilla~\cite{pelkonen2015gorilla} and
ALP~\cite{afroozeh2024alp} are lossless; SZ3~\cite{liang2023sz3} and recent
error-bounded time-series compressors~\cite{shrink2025} guarantee a uniform
pointwise bound. CAMEO~\cite{cameo2025} bounds the deviation of
autocorrelation functions, and Muñiz-Cuza et
al.~\cite{munizcuza2024forecasting} measure the effect of error-bounded
compression on forecasting. None of them bounds discrete decisions.
Industrial approaches (e.g.\ aggregating series that no alert or dashboard
reads) select series rather than bound values. \todo{novelty search: DBLP /
Scholar 2024--2026 for alert-aware, decision-preserving, rule-aware
downsampling, SLO-aware telemetry; vendor patents (Datadog, Grafana Labs,
Chronosphere, Splunk, Dynatrace).}
